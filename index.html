The problem

Most small sites
cost more than they return

A site built on a drag-and-drop platform in 2019 is still charging a licence fee, still loading slowly on a phone, and still sending enquiries to an inbox nobody checks. It is a leaflet on a shelf. A working site does a job every week.

None of these are design problems. They are the six things that decide whether a small business site earns back what it costs, and they are worth checking on the site you have now.

Slow on a phone

Four seconds to load and most of the visit is gone before your name appears

Invisible in search

No page per service and no local terms, so it never shows for the work you want

Enquiries that vanish

A form that quietly fails, or lands in a platform inbox nobody logs into

Rented, not owned

Stop paying and the site goes with it, design, content and all

Paying for nothing

A licence fee every month whether the site brought in work or not

Nobody to ask

A support queue and a help centre, instead of the person who built it

Questions

The things people ask before they commit

How long does a site take?
A one page site takes about a week. A five page business site takes 2 to 3 weeks from the day the content is agreed. The slow part is usually photos and wording, so I draft both for you if that helps.
Will I lose my Google ranking if you rebuild the site?
No. Old addresses are redirected to the new ones, the page titles and headings are carried over, and the site is submitted to Google the day it goes live. Pages usually get faster, which helps rather than hurts.
Can I edit the site myself?
Yes, where it makes sense. Prices, opening hours, galleries and testimonials can sit behind a simple admin screen you log into. Most owners would rather send a message and have the change made for them, which a plan covers.
What happens to my email address?
Nothing. Email and the website are separate records on your domain. Your address keeps working through the switch, and I check it before and after the site goes live.
What happens after the first 3 months?
The build price covers the first 3 months on the Care plan. After that it continues at £45 a month unless you tell me to stop, and stopping costs you nothing: the site, the domain, the code and the logins are yours either way. The plan buys hosting, backups, monitoring and changes made for you, not permission to keep using your own site.
Where do you work?
Hampshire and Surrey for anything face to face. Everywhere else in the UK by phone and email, which is how most of the work runs anyway.
