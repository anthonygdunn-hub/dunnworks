About

You deal with the person who writes the code

Dunnworks is one person, Tony Dunn, working with small businesses in Hampshire, Surrey and further out. No account manager, no team you never meet, and no work handed to someone else once the invoice is agreed.

The short version

Small businesses were sold websites that were never built for them

Nearly every small business I speak to is on the same footing. A site was built on a platform 5 years ago, it has been paid for every month since, and it has never brought in a single job anybody can point to. Nobody is happy with it and nobody wants the argument of replacing it.

That is the work. Take what the business already has, write the site by hand so it loads fast and reads properly on a phone, put the phone number and the enquiry form where people can reach them, and hand the whole thing over so it belongs to the owner rather than a platform.

How the sites are made

The tools are boring on purpose

Nothing here is chosen because it is new. It is chosen because it is cheap to run, easy to move, and still going to work in 5 years.

Front end

HTML, CSS and JavaScript

Written by hand, with no build step and no framework. The files you are handed are the files the browser reads.

Hosting

Static hosting on a fast network

GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages. Free for a small business site, quick everywhere, and simple to move.

Data

Supabase, only where it earns its place

Galleries, testimonials, members and enquiry records. A proper database rather than a monthly plugin.

Email

Resend and Web3Forms

Enquiries reach your inbox and stay out of spam, with a copy kept so nothing is ever lost.

Search

Google Business Profile and Search Console

Set up at launch, with a sitemap and structured data, so the site can be found for the work you want.

Handover

A GitHub repository in your name

Every change is recorded and reversible. If you ever move to someone else, they get the lot.

Good fit

Who this suits

  • trades and small firms with 1 to 20 people
  • consultants and one-person practices
  • clubs, societies and groups with members to manage
  • anyone paying monthly for a site that does nothing
  • owners who would rather ask a person than log into a dashboard
Poor fit

Who this does not suit

  • shops needing hundreds of products and stock control
  • anyone who wants the cheapest possible site
  • work that needs a full marketing team behind it
  • projects where nobody can spare an hour for a call

If that is you, say so and I will point you at someone better suited. It costs nothing to ask.
