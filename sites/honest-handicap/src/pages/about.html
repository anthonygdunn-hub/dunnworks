About Honest Handicap

Most golf kit reviews come from tour pros, testing robots or people who've never had to fix a slice on a wet Sunday in February. Ours come from club golfers.

Honest Handicap started with a group of society golfers in Hampshire, from the Falkners Arms Golf Society, who were tired of buying kit on the strength of reviews written for players nothing like them. So every review here shows the tester's handicap, their home course and how many rounds they played, and asks one simple question: would you buy it again with your own money?

"I've bought kit because a review told me to, and found out on the first tee that the reviewer played a different game to me. I wanted reviews from people who play like the rest of us, in the weather the rest of us play in. So I asked the golfers I play with to write down what they really think of their kit. That's all this is."

Anthony Dunn, founder

What we promise

  • Every review comes from a real tester who played with the kit. No made-up reviews, ever
  • The tester's verdict is their own. We fix spelling, not opinions
  • Free or loaned kit is declared at the top of the review
  • Buy now links are affiliate links, always marked (Ad), and they're added after the score, never before

Fitting and regrips

We're not a shop and we don't fit clubs. When testers want kit fitted or regripped, we send them to Coastal Golf Co, who collect from your door from Cranleigh to the coast.
