Privacy and cookies

The short version: we only keep what you send us, we only show your first name, handicap and home course, and this site doesn't track you.

Who we are

Honest Handicap is run by Dunnworks (Anthony Dunn), based in Bordon, Hampshire, which is the data controller for anything you send us. Contact: hello@honesthandicap.golf.

What we collect, and why

  • If you apply to be a tester: your name, email, handicap, home course, how often you play and what's in your bag. We use it to decide on applications, send you your tester code and keep in touch about testing. Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in running the tester panel, which you've asked to join
  • If you hand in a review card: your card, any photos you add, and the handicap you give. We publish the review with your first name, handicap and home course, which you agreed to when you joined. Lawful basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time
  • Spam protection: when a form is sent we keep a scrambled (hashed) version of your connection's address for a short time so we can limit repeated sends. We can't turn it back into your IP address

We never sell your details or pass them to brands or retailers.

Who helps us run it

  • Supabase stores the forms, cards and photos
  • Resend sends our emails
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts the site uses, so your browser asks Google for them

Some of these companies may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use the safeguards UK law requires, such as the UK Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Tester details are kept while you're a tester and deleted within six months of you leaving. Cards that aren't published are deleted within twelve months. Published reviews stay up unless you ask us to take yours down.

Cookies

This site doesn't set any cookies of its own and doesn't use analytics that track you. When you click a Buy now (Ad) link, the retailer or affiliate network may set a cookie so they know the sale came from us. That's covered by their own privacy and cookie policies.

Your rights

You can ask to see what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or take down a review you wrote. Email us and we'll do it within a month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
