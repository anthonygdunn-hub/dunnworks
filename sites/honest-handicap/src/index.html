How we test

Every review on Honest Handicap comes from a real club golfer who has played with the kit. Here's the card they all fill in.

{{STEPS}}

The scorecard

  • The tester's first name, handicap, home course and how often they play
  • Where the kit came from and what they paid
  • Rounds and range sessions played with it
  • Scores out of 10 for performance, feel, build quality and value
  • Would they buy it again with their own money? Yes or no
  • Who it suits, who it doesn't, and one thing that annoyed them
  • An overall verdict out of 10, circled in pencil

Free kit

If a brand lends or gives us something to test, the review says so at the top. The verdict is still the tester's own, and a brand never sees a review before it's published.

About Honest Handicap

Most golf kit reviews come from tour pros, testing robots or people who've never had to fix a slice on a wet Sunday in February. Ours come from club golfers.

Honest Handicap started with a group of society golfers in Hampshire who were tired of buying kit on the strength of reviews written for players nothing like them. So every review here shows the tester's handicap, their home course and how many rounds they played, and asks one simple question: would you buy it again with your own money?
