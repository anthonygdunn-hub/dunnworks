Case study · Rebuild with a database

T Cooper Interiors

Kitchen and bathroom fitters in Farnborough, with 954 Facebook followers and years of job photos that never reached the website.

Rebuild7 servicesGalleryTestimonials

The brief

Replace a platform site and use the photos

The old site was slow, the gallery was out of date, and the best proof of the work sat in Facebook posts nobody visiting the site would ever see.

What was built

A site backed by a database

Enquiries, testimonials and the gallery all run from a database, so they can change without touching the code. New enquiries alert by email the moment they arrive.

Where it stands

Off the old platform for good

Photos were moved off the old platform's image service on 1 September 2026, so the previous site could be closed without the pictures disappearing.
