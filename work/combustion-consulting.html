Combustion Consulting

An engine consultancy with 50 years in industrial diesel, rebuilt from a 4 page platform site that said almost none of it.

Rebuild5 pagesEnquiry form

The brief

Keep the words, lose the platform

Keep the existing wording, colours and the founder's photo. Add the career record that made the business worth hiring. Stop the monthly fee.

What was built

Five pages and a track record

Home, services, track record, about and contact, in the existing black and lime, with a moving hero, counters and reveals. Enquiries go to the company address.

Where it stands

Live since 3 September 2026

The business is pre-launch, so the results page sets out career roles and the measures each service moves, rather than claiming client figures it does not have yet.
