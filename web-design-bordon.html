Why it matters here

The town is getting bigger, and so is the competition for the same customers

The Whitehill and Bordon regeneration is a 15 year programme to build 3,350 homes and create 3,350 jobs by 2036, with the population going from about 14,000 to 22,000 by 2030 and nearly 100,000 square metres of new commercial, retail and leisure space. Thousands of people who have never heard of your business are moving in, and every one of them will find their tradesman, their garage and their hairdresser the same way: by searching.

New arrivals search, they do not ask around

Word of mouth built most businesses in this town, and it still works. It does not reach somebody who moved into a new build three weeks ago. A site that names Bordon, names what you do and loads on a phone is how those people find you instead of a firm in Alton.

A face, not a helpdesk

I live in Bordon. That means a meeting in person, photos taken on site if the ones you have are not good enough, and one person who answers the phone rather than a ticket queue. It is also why there is no monthly fee: the site is finished, it is yours, and I am still down the road if something needs changing.
