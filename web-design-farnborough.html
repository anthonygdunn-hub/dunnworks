The local job

What the rebuild actually changed for a Farnborough trade

T Cooper Interiors had 954 followers on Facebook and a website that showed none of the work those followers had already seen. Every finished kitchen lived in a post that disappeared down a feed after a week. The rebuild put the gallery, the testimonials and the enquiry form on a database, so adding a job takes a few minutes and the work stays on the site for good.

The photos were already there

Most trades in Farnborough are sitting on the same asset: a phone full of finished jobs. Getting them onto a page search engines can read is usually a bigger win than any amount of rewriting.

Aldershot and Cove count as Farnborough

People search by the town they think they are in. A page naming Farnborough, Aldershot and Cove catches all three, which a page built around one postcode does not.
