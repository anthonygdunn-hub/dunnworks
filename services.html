Service 02

A rebuild keeps what works and fixes what does not

This is most of the work I do. The business is fine, the wording is mostly right, and the site is 5 years old on a platform that charges monthly. Nothing has to be thrown away.

How the switch is handled

  • every page and photo copied across before anything changes
  • old addresses redirected, so search results and old links still work
  • email left untouched and checked before and after
  • the new site is built on a private link while the old one stays up
  • the change over happens in minutes, usually in an evening

What normally changes

  • pages load in under a second instead of 4 or 5
  • the phone number and enquiry form are reachable on every screen
  • each service gets its own page, so it can be found in search
  • the monthly platform fee stops
  • you get the files, so the next person can pick it up

From £950.
