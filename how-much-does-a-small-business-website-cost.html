How much does a small business website cost in the UK?

A straight answer, not a "contact us for pricing" page. Here is what the options actually cost, what changes the number, and where the ongoing fees hide.

The short answer

Three realistic price bands

DIY platform builder

Wix, Squarespace or GoDaddy. £0 to build it yourself, but £17 to £45 a month forever, plus your own time to write and design it.

Freelancer or small agency

Typically £750 to £3,000 for a small business site, one-off, sometimes with a monthly hosting or maintenance add-on.

Larger agency

£3,000 upward, often £5,000+, aimed at businesses that need a marketing team behind the site rather than a working brochure.

What changes the price

Four things that move the number

Number of pages

A single scrolling page costs less than a five page site with a page for every service, because there is less to write and design.

New build vs rebuild

A rebuild reuses existing wording and photos, so it is usually cheaper than starting from nothing, even though redirects and care around the switch add work.

Bookings, galleries or logins

A booking form, a self-updating gallery or a members area with its own admin screen are quoted on top, because each is effectively a small piece of software.

Who writes the words

Wording drafted for you costs more up front than supplying finished copy yourself, but usually costs less than getting it wrong and paying to redo it later.

The hidden number

The monthly fee matters more than the build price

A £0 platform builder that charges £30 a month costs £1,080 over three years, more than a £950 fixed-price rebuild with no ongoing licence fee. The build price is only half the sum. Always ask what the site costs to keep running, not just what it costs to launch.
