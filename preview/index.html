How these work

Locked properly, not just hidden

A preview is not simply an unlisted address. Each one is encrypted, so until the passphrase is entered there is nothing on the page to read, even in the source.

You get a link and a passphrase

Sent separately, usually the link by email and the passphrase by text, so one intercepted message on its own opens nothing.

The page decrypts in your browser

AES-256 with the key derived from your passphrase. Nothing is sent anywhere and no account is needed. Any modern browser will do.

Look around, then tell me what to change

Every link, gallery and form works. Some figures are drafts, marked as such, waiting on your confirmation.
