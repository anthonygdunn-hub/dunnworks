Cookie policy

What this site stores, and why

Short version: nothing that tracks you. The site remembers your theme and your cookie choice, and that is all. Last updated 20 September 2026.

Necessary storage

Always on, because the site needs it to remember what you asked it to.

  • dw-theme: your light or dark theme choice, kept until you clear it.
  • dw-consent: the cookie choice you made, so this banner does not ask again.

Both are kept in your own browser's local storage. They are never sent to me or anyone else.

Analytics and marketing

None are running at the moment. If I add visitor analytics or advert measurement in future, it will only load after you choose "Accept all" or switch that category on under "Customise", and this page will be updated to name the service.

Other services the site uses

  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser fetches them from Google, which sees your IP address as part of any web request. Google Fonts does not set cookies.
  • Web3Forms receives the enquiry form, only when you press send, so it can forward your message by email.
  • WhatsApp links open WhatsApp only if you tap them.

Changing your mind

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time.

You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings. Questions to info@dunnworks.io.
